# Claude "Ash" Ashby: RCAF

A family history site for Claude Ashby (17 April 1921 – 5 March 1998), a southern Alberta boy who flew 51 operational trips with the RAF's Western Desert Air Force and made a career in the Royal Canadian Air Force.

Live site: https://inottawa.github.io/claude-ashby/

## Editing

- The site is plain HTML and CSS: `index.html` and `style.css`. There's no build step.
- Text marked `class="pending"` is a placeholder waiting on family details or his service file. Replace the text and remove the class once it's confirmed.
- Put photos in `images/`.
- Add a source in the **Records & sources** section for every new fact.

### Not a match

The 1921 census lists a Claude Ashby, age 13, in Strathcona, AB. That's a different person.

### Kept off the public site on purpose

The death registration also shows his health number, his street address, and the informant's name and address. None of these are published on the site.

### Still to find

His RCAF service file, which would give his service number, trade, squadrons, aircraft, medals, postings and retirement date. Request it from Library and Archives Canada, using the BC death registration as proof of death.
