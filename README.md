# Claude Ashby: RCAF

A family history site for Claude Ashby (born 17 April 1921, Lethbridge, Alberta) and his service with the Royal Canadian Air Force.

Live site: https://inottawa.github.io/claude-ashby/

## Editing

- The site is plain HTML and CSS: `index.html` and `style.css`. There's no build step.
- Text marked `class="pending"` is a placeholder waiting on family details. Replace the text and remove the class once it's confirmed.
- Put photos in `images/` and reference them as `images/<file>.jpg`.
- Add a source in the **Records & sources** section for every new fact.

The LAC search also returns a **1921 census entry for a Claude Ashby, age 13, in Strathcona, Alberta**. That's a different person, because our Claude was born in April 1921.

**Still to find:** his RCAF service file. Second World War files for people who survived the war aren't online. Request a copy from Library and Archives Canada with his name, date of birth and proof of death.
